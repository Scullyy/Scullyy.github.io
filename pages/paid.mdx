## FAQ
<details>
<summary>Where do I download my purchased resources?</summary>

Sign in to the [Cfx.re Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets) with the same Cfx.re account you used at checkout and open **Assets → Granted Assets**. Every resource you own is listed there with a download option.

If a purchase is missing, make sure you are signed in to the correct Cfx.re account. Assets can take a few minutes to appear after payment.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I transfer an asset I purchased?</summary>

Assets are transferred from the Cfx.re Portal:

1. Sign in to the [Cfx.re Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets) and go to **Assets → Granted Assets**.
2. Find the asset you want to move and choose the **Transfer** option for it.
3. Enter the details of the Cfx.re account you want to transfer the asset to and confirm.
4. Follow any confirmation steps the portal asks for. Once confirmed, the asset will appear in the other account's Granted Assets.

Please note that once a transfer is complete, it cannot be reversed.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I fix the "you lack the required entitlement" error?</summary>

This means the resource is linked to a different Cfx.re account than the one that owns your server's license key. Escrowed resources only run on servers whose key belongs to the account that owns the asset.

To fix it, either transfer the asset to the account that owns your server key (see the question above), or generate your server key from the account that owns the asset in the [Cfx.re Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/).

If you are unable to transfer the asset, we will not be able to provide further assistance as the asset may have already been transferred. If you suspect that your Cfx.re account has been compromised, change your password immediately and contact Cfx.re support via https://support.cfx.re/hc/en-us/requests/new to report the incident and request the assets be returned.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I fix the syntax error near '&lt;\1>' error?</summary>

This almost always means the escrowed files were damaged when they were uploaded to your server:

- **FileZilla** can disrupt the file structure of escrowed resources. Use [WinSCP](https://winscp.net/) instead.
- **Drag and drop over Remote Desktop** can also corrupt files. Upload the resource as a zip and extract it on the server instead.

It can also mean your server artifacts are outdated. Make sure you are running the latest recommended server build.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I fix the "error parsing script" or "failed to load script" error?</summary>

Your server artifacts are most likely outdated. Our resources are built for current FiveM builds, so older artifacts can fail to load them.

Update your server to the **latest recommended** artifacts from the official [Windows](https://runtime.fivem.net/artifacts/fivem/build_server_windows/master/) or [Linux](https://runtime.fivem.net/artifacts/fivem/build_proot_linux/master/) artifact pages, or use your hosting provider's update option. Keeping your artifacts up to date also improves performance, stability and security.
</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I fix the "failed to verify protected resource" error?</summary>

The resource's files were changed or are incomplete, usually because of how they were transferred to the server.

- Make sure **hidden files** are copied. Some FTP clients skip them by default.
- Make sure the **`.fxap` file** is included. Escrowed resources cannot load without it.
- Use a reliable transfer method such as **SFTP** or **SCP**, or upload a zip and extract it on the server.
- Do not edit escrowed files. Only the files listed as editable (such as the config) can be changed.

If the error persists, delete the resource from your server, download a fresh copy from the [Cfx.re Portal](https://portal.cfx.re/assets/granted-assets) and upload it again.
</details>
